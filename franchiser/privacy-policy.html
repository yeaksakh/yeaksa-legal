Privacy Policy

Franchiser mobile application (iOS bundle ID com.yeaksa.franchiser) · Effective date: 20 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yeaksa ("Yeaksa", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use the Franchiser mobile application (the "App"). Franchiser is the staff attendance app for businesses and franchise branches that run on the Mekhea business platform at mekhea.com, operated by Yeaksa. Employees use it to clock in and out, and to see their own attendance history and payroll. It is a work tool, not a consumer app. By installing or using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

In short: you sign in with the staff account your business created on mekhea.com. When you clock in or out, the App sends the time, a selfie taken with the front camera at that moment, your location and an optional note to your business's records on the mekhea.com servers. We do not sell personal data, the App contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, location is never collected in the background, and you can have your account and data deleted (see Deleting your account).

1. Who we are

The App is operated by Yeaksa, a business-software company based in Khan Russey Keo, Phnom Penh, Cambodia. Yeaksa provides the Mekhea servers and the App to businesses ("your business") that hold a Mekhea account. Your business decides which of its staff may sign in and uses the attendance records for its own staff management; Yeaksa processes that data to provide the service. Contact details are in the Contact us section.

2. Data we collect

You cannot use the App without an account: a business owner or manager creates staff accounts on mekhea.com and gives you a username and password. The App only shows and records data belonging to you and your business.

3. App permissions

The App asks for these permissions when it is first opened. Each one can be refused or turned off later in your device settings:

  • Camera – to show the live selfie preview in the attendance panel and take the attendance selfie when you press Clock In or Clock Out. If you refuse, you can still clock in and out without a photo, unless your business requires one.
  • Location (while using the App) – to attach your position to each clock-in and clock-out. If you refuse, you can still clock in and out, unless your business requires location.
  • Internet – to connect to the Mekhea servers.

4. How we use your data

  • To sign you in and show your name, status and records.
  • To record your attendance, with the selfie, location and note, and show you your attendance history and payroll.
  • To keep the App secure, prevent fraud and abuse (for example someone clocking in for a colleague), and enforce our terms.
  • To comply with legal obligations such as labour and tax law.

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

5. Who we share data with

We never sell personal data. Data is shared only as follows:

  • Your business – the owner and managers of the business you work for can see your attendance records, including the selfies, locations and notes, and your payroll, on mekhea.com. This is the purpose of the service.
  • Service providers – companies that host our servers. They may only use data to provide the service to us.
  • Legal requirements – if required by law, court order or a competent authority, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Yeaksa, our users or others.
  • Business transfers – if Yeaksa is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

6. How long we keep data

  • Your staff account is kept while it is active in your business's Mekhea account. When it is deleted, your profile is removed from our active systems within 30 days.
  • Attendance and payroll records belong to your business and are kept for as long as the business keeps its Mekhea account, and thereafter for up to 10 years where tax, accounting or labour law requires, in a form no longer linked to a usable staff profile.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 90 days for security and troubleshooting.
  • Data stored on your phone is removed when you sign out (except the Remember me details, if you chose them) or uninstall the App.

7. Security

All traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are stored on our servers only as salted hashes. Access to personal data is restricted to people who need it to run the service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data using appropriate technical and organisational measures.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access – you can see your attendance history and payroll in the App at any time. Your account details are managed by your business's administrator on mekhea.com.
  • Permissions – turn off camera or location access at any time in your device settings.
  • Delete – ask your business's administrator to delete your staff account, or contact us (see below).
  • Other rights – depending on where you live you may have further rights, including to receive a copy of your data or to object to certain processing. To exercise any right, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days and may ask you to verify your identity first. Because attendance records are controlled by your business, some requests will be passed to it for a decision.

9. Deleting your account

By your business: the owner or a manager of your business can delete your staff account at any time on mekhea.com. Your sign-in stops immediately and your personal data is erased from our active systems within 30 days.

By request to us: email myyeaksa@gmail.com from an address linked to your account, or write to us with the username and business name of your account, and ask us to delete it. We will verify your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Attendance and payroll records that your business or the law requires to be kept (see section 6) stay with the business without your usable profile attached. Everything else about you is deleted.

10. Children

The App is a work tool and is not directed at children. You must be at least 18 years old to hold a staff account. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located outside your country, including in countries that do not offer the same level of data protection as your own. Whenever we transfer data internationally we take steps to ensure it remains protected in line with this policy.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. If the changes are significant we will notify you in the App before they take effect. Continued use of the App after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.
