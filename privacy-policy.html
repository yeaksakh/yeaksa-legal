Privacy Policy

Yeaksa Shop mobile application (Android package com.yeaksashop.yeaksa) · Effective date: 16 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yeaksa ("Yeaksa", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use the Yeaksa Shop mobile application (the "App") and the services available through it, including our online store at yeaksa.com. By installing or using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

In short: we collect the information needed to create your account, deliver your orders and support you. We do not sell your personal data, and the App contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. You can delete your account and data from inside the App at any time (see Deleting your account).

1. Who we are

The App is operated by Yeaksa, an online marketplace based in Khan Russey Keo, Phnom Penh, Cambodia. Yeaksa is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. Contact details are in the Contact us section.

3. App permissions

The App may ask for the following Android permissions. Each one is optional and only requested when you use the related feature:

  • Location (fine/coarse, while in use) – to pin a delivery address on the map.
  • Camera and photos/media – to choose or take a profile photo, attach evidence to a refund request or send an image in chat. The system picker is used, so we only receive the files you select.
  • Storage – to save digital products you purchase and files shared in chat to your device.
  • Notifications – to show order updates and offers. You can turn these off in your device settings at any time.
  • Internet / network state – to connect to our servers and show you when you are offline.

4. How we use your data

  • To create and manage your account and verify your phone number.
  • To process payments, fulfil and deliver orders, and handle returns and refunds.
  • To send you order confirmations, delivery updates, OTP codes and replies to your support requests.
  • To send promotional notifications about offers, flash deals and new products, if you have notifications enabled. You may opt out at any time (see Your rights and choices).
  • To operate features you choose to use, such as chat with sellers, wishlist, wallet, club points, auctions and classified ads.
  • To keep the App secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our terms.
  • To comply with legal obligations such as tax, accounting and consumer-protection law.
  • To improve the App based on how its features are used, using aggregated information that does not identify you.

5. Who we share data with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only as follows:

  • Sellers on the marketplace – when you buy from a seller, they receive the name, phone number and delivery address needed to fulfil that order, plus any chat messages you send them.
  • Delivery partners – receive the recipient name, phone number and address needed to deliver your parcel.
  • Payment providers – when you pay online, the provider you select processes your payment under its own privacy policy. We receive only the transaction status and reference.
  • Service providers – companies that host our servers and send push notifications on our behalf (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging). They may only use your data to provide the service to us.
  • Legal requirements – if required by law, court order or a competent authority, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Yeaksa, our users or others.
  • Business transfers – if Yeaksa is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data is kept while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, addresses, wishlist, chat messages and push token are removed from our active systems within 30 days.
  • Order, invoice and payment records may be kept for up to 10 years after the transaction where required by tax and accounting law, even after account deletion, in a form that is no longer linked to a usable profile.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 90 days for security and troubleshooting.

7. Security

All traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Access to personal data is restricted to staff who need it to run the service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data using appropriate technical and organisational measures.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access and update – you can view and edit your name, phone number, email, photo and addresses at any time in the App under Profile.
  • Notifications – disable them in your device's app settings, or in the App under Settings.
  • Permissions – revoke location, camera or storage access at any time in your device settings; the related feature will stop working but the rest of the App will continue to function.
  • Delete – delete your account and data from within the App (see below) or by contacting us.
  • Portability, restriction and objection – depending on where you live you may have further rights, including to receive a copy of your data or to object to certain processing. To exercise any right, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days and may ask you to verify your identity first.

9. Deleting your account

From inside the App: open Profile → Settings → Delete account and confirm. Your account is deactivated immediately and your personal data is erased from our active systems within 30 days.

By request: if you can no longer sign in, email us at myyeaksa@gmail.com from the address on your account, or contact us with the phone number registered to your account, and ask us to delete it. We will verify your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Order and invoice records that we are legally required to retain (see section 6) are kept for the statutory period and then destroyed. Everything else is deleted.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and you must be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority where you live) to place an order. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located outside your country, including in countries that do not offer the same level of data protection as your own. Whenever we transfer data internationally we take steps to ensure it remains protected in line with this policy.

12. Third-party links

The App may open external websites or apps – for example a seller's page, a payment provider, a video hosted on YouTube, or a WhatsApp conversation with a seller. Those services have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for how they handle your data.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. If the changes are significant we will notify you in the App or by notification before they take effect. Continued use of the App after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.
