Privacy Policy

Mekhea (មេឃា) mobile application (Android package com.mekhea.mekhea) · Effective date: 24 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yeaksa ("Yeaksa", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use the Mekhea mobile application (the "App"). Mekhea lets the owners and staff of businesses that run on the Mekhea platform (mekhea.com) use it on their phone: their profile, the point of sale, follow-ups, expense requests and the other tools their business has turned on. By installing or using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

In short: the App opens your business's Mekhea account at mekhea.com. It keeps you signed in on your phone until you log out, stores your sign-in only on your device in encrypted form, and sends nothing to anyone except mekhea.com. We do not sell your personal data, and the App contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

1. Who we are

The App is operated by Yeaksa, a business platform based in Khan Russey Keo, Phnom Penh, Cambodia. Yeaksa is the data controller for the account and technical data described in this policy. The business that you work for controls its own business records (customers, sales, products, expenses) that you see and enter in the App.

2. Data we collect

You cannot use the App without an account: a business owner or manager creates staff accounts on mekhea.com and gives you a username and password. The App shows the same pages you would see at mekhea.com in a browser, so it can only show and change what your account is allowed to.

3. App permissions

The App may ask for the following Android permissions. Each one is only needed for the feature described and can be refused; the rest of the App keeps working:

  • Camera / photos – to scan a barcode or take or choose a photo to upload. The system picker is used, so we only receive the picture or file you select.
  • Internet – to connect to mekhea.com.

4. How we use your data

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in on your device until you log out.
  • To run the business tools your business uses: point of sale, follow-ups, expense requests, tasks and reports.
  • To keep the App secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our terms.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

5. Who we share data with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only as follows:

  • Your business – records you create become part of your business's records, visible to its owners and managers like any other record. That is the purpose of the service.
  • Service providers – companies that host our servers on our behalf. They may only use your data to provide the service to us.
  • Legal requirements – if required by law, court order or a competent authority, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Yeaksa, our users or others.
  • Business transfers – if Yeaksa is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data is kept while your staff account exists. When it is deleted, your profile and sign-in details are removed from our active systems within 30 days.
  • The sign-in saved on your phone is deleted when you tap Logout or uninstall the App.
  • Records your business is legally required to retain (such as invoices) are kept by the business for the statutory period; they are not part of your account.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 90 days for security and troubleshooting.

7. Security

All traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are stored on our server only as salted hashes, and the sign-in kept on your phone is encrypted by the operating system's secure storage. The server checks your permissions on every request. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data using appropriate technical and organisational measures.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access and update – your profile is shown in the App. To change it, ask your business's manager or contact us.
  • Permissions – revoke camera or photo access at any time in your device settings.
  • Log out – tap Logout to end your session and delete the sign-in saved on that phone.
  • Delete – ask for your account to be deleted (see below).
  • Portability, restriction and objection – depending on where you live you may have further rights, including to receive a copy of your data or to object to certain processing. To exercise any right, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days and may ask you to verify your identity first.

9. Deleting your account

Staff accounts are created and managed by your business, so deletion is handled by request rather than from inside the App.

By request: ask your business's owner or manager to delete your staff account on mekhea.com, or email us at myyeaksa@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will verify your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Records you created are part of your business's records and stay with the business; they are no longer linked to a usable profile once your account is gone.

10. Children

The App is intended for adult business owners and staff only. You must be at least 18 years old to hold an account. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located outside your country, including in countries that do not offer the same level of data protection as your own. Whenever we transfer data internationally we take steps to ensure it remains protected in line with this policy.

12. Third-party links

The App shows mekhea.com. Links to other websites or apps (for example a phone number, an email address or a map) open outside the App in the app you choose; those services have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for how they handle your data.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. If the changes are significant we will notify you in the App before they take effect. Continued use of the App after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.
