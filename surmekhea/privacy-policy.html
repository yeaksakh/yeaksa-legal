Privacy Policy

SurMekhea (សួរមេខា) mobile application (Android package com.yeaksa.surmekhea) · Effective date: 18 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yeaksa ("Yeaksa", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use the SurMekhea mobile application (the "App"). SurMekhea lets the staff of businesses that run on the Yeaksa platform (yeaksa.com) ask the platform's assistant questions about their own business – by typing, by speaking, or by sending a picture – and confirm or reject the changes the assistant proposes. It is intended for those staff, not for shoppers. By installing or using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

In short: you sign in with the staff account your business created on yeaksa.com. Your questions, voice recordings and pictures are sent to the Yeaksa servers to be answered, and the answers come only from data your role may already see. The assistant never changes anything without your explicit confirmation. We do not sell personal data, the App contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, and you can have your account and conversations deleted at any time (see Deleting your account).

1. Who we are

The App is operated by Yeaksa, an online marketplace and business platform based in Khan Russey Keo, Phnom Penh, Cambodia. Yeaksa is the data controller for the account, conversation and technical data described in this policy. The business that employs you is the controller of the business records the assistant reads and, with your confirmation, writes; Yeaksa processes them on its behalf. Contact details are in the Contact us section.

2. Data we collect

You cannot use the App without an account: a business owner or manager creates staff accounts on yeaksa.com and gives you a username and password. Every request the App makes carries your own sign-in, so the assistant can only see and do what you could see and do on the website.

3. App permissions

The App may ask for the following Android permissions. Each one is only needed for the feature described and can be refused; the rest of the App keeps working:

  • Microphone – to record a spoken question while you hold the button. Nothing is recorded at any other time.
  • Camera / photos – to take or choose a picture to send with a question. The system camera and picker are used, so we only receive the picture you take or select.
  • Internet – to connect to your business's server.

4. How we use your data

  • To sign you in and keep your session on your device.
  • To transcribe your voice, understand your question and picture, and answer from your business's records.
  • To keep your threads so you can return to them, and to hold proposed changes until you decide.
  • To improve the assistant's answers using your feedback and aggregated usage that does not identify you.
  • To keep the App secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our terms.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

5. Who we share data with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only as follows:

  • Your business – changes you confirm become part of your business's records, visible to its owners and managers like any other record. That is the purpose of the service.
  • Service providers – companies that host our servers and, where the assistant relies on them, providers of speech-to-text and language-model services that process your question, recording or picture on our behalf. They may only use your data to provide the service to us and may not use it for their own purposes.
  • Legal requirements – if required by law, court order or a competent authority, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Yeaksa, our users or others.
  • Business transfers – if Yeaksa is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data is kept while your staff account exists. When it is deleted, your profile, sign-in details and device sessions are removed from our active systems within 30 days.
  • Conversations and feedback are kept with your account; archiving a thread hides it, deleting your account removes them.
  • Voice recordings are deleted once transcribed; only the text is kept with the conversation.
  • Records your business is legally required to retain are kept by the business for the statutory period; they are not part of your account.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 90 days for security and troubleshooting.

7. Security

All traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, and your session token is stored on your device and revoked when you sign out. The server checks your permissions on every request, so the assistant can never read or write more than you could yourself, and it writes nothing until you confirm. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data using appropriate technical and organisational measures.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access and update – your name and role are shown in the App. To change them, ask your business's manager or contact us.
  • Permissions – revoke microphone or camera access at any time in your device settings; you can still ask by typing.
  • Threads – rename or archive any thread from the drawer.
  • Sign out – sign out from the drawer to end the session on that device and revoke its token on the server.
  • Delete – ask for your account and conversations to be deleted (see below).
  • Portability, restriction and objection – depending on where you live you may have further rights, including to receive a copy of your data or to object to certain processing. To exercise any right, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days and may ask you to verify your identity first.

9. Deleting your account

Staff accounts are created and managed by your business, so deletion is handled by request rather than from inside the App.

By request: ask your business's owner or manager to delete your staff account on yeaksa.com, or email us at myyeaksa@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will verify your identity, deactivate the account immediately and erase your personal data – including your conversations, feedback and any pictures you sent – from our active systems within 30 days. You can also sign out of the App at any time, which ends the session on your device.

Changes you confirmed are part of your business's records and stay with the business; they are no longer linked to a usable profile once your account is gone.

10. Children

The App is intended for adult staff only. You must be at least 18 years old to hold a staff account. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Our servers and service providers, including speech-to-text and language-model providers, may be located outside your country, including in countries that do not offer the same level of data protection as your own. Whenever we transfer data internationally we take steps to ensure it remains protected in line with this policy.

12. Third-party links

The App connects only to the server your business runs on (yeaksa.com or mekhea.com) and contains no links to third-party websites or services.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. If the changes are significant we will notify you in the App before they take effect. Continued use of the App after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.
