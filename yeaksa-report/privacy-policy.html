Privacy Policy

YeaksaReport (របាយការណ៍យក្សា) mobile application (Android package com.yeaksa.yeaksareport) · Effective date: 17 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yeaksa ("Yeaksa", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use the YeaksaReport mobile application (the "App"). YeaksaReport shows the staff of businesses that run on the Yeaksa platform (yeaksa.com) the reports their business already keeps there – sales, stock, finance, HR and more – with filters, date ranges and totals, and lets them export a report as a spreadsheet. It is intended for those staff, not for shoppers. By installing or using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

In short: you sign in with the staff account your business created on yeaksa.com, and the App only reads your business's reports from the Yeaksa servers. It writes nothing except which reports you mark as favourites. We do not sell personal data, the App contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, and you can have your account deleted at any time (see Deleting your account).

1. Who we are

The App is operated by Yeaksa, an online marketplace and business platform based in Khan Russey Keo, Phnom Penh, Cambodia. Yeaksa is the data controller for the account and technical data described in this policy. The business that employs you is the controller of the records its reports are built from; Yeaksa processes them on its behalf. Contact details are in the Contact us section.

2. Data we collect

You cannot use the App without an account: a business owner or manager creates staff accounts on yeaksa.com and gives you a username and password. The App then only shows reports belonging to that business, and only those your role is allowed to see.

CategoryWhat we collectWhy
Account informationYour username, password (stored only in hashed form), display name, role and the business you belong to, and an access token that keeps you signed in on your device.To sign you in and show you the reports your role allows.
Report usageWhich reports you open, the filters and date ranges you choose, and the reports you mark as favourites.To run the report you asked for and remember your favourites.
Exports (on device)A spreadsheet the App generates from a report when you tap Export. It is written to your device's storage and handed to the app you choose to share it with. Yeaksa does not receive a copy.To let you keep or send a report.

The reports themselves contain your business's operating data – sales, stock, expenses, staff attendance and so on – which may include personal data about customers and staff. That data is collected and controlled by your business on yeaksa.com; the App only displays it. We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, files, call logs, messages, advertising identifiers or biometric data. The App contains no third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

3. App permissions

The App requests only the Internet permission, to connect to your business's server. Exporting a report uses the Android share sheet, so no storage permission is needed and the App never reads your files.

4. How we use your data

  • To sign you in and keep your session on your device.
  • To fetch and display the reports you open, with the filters you choose, and to generate an export when you ask for one.
  • To remember your favourite reports.
  • To keep the App secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our terms.
  • To comply with legal obligations.
  • To improve the App using aggregated information that does not identify you.

5. Who we share data with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only as follows:

  • Your business – the owners and managers of the business you work for can see, on yeaksa.com, that your account exists and which role it has. That is the purpose of the service.
  • Apps you share an export with – when you export a report, the spreadsheet goes to whichever app you pick in the share sheet, under that app's own privacy policy.
  • Service providers – companies that host our servers. They may only use your data to provide the service to us.
  • Legal requirements – if required by law, court order or a competent authority, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Yeaksa, our users or others.
  • Business transfers – if Yeaksa is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data is kept while your staff account exists. When it is deleted, your profile, sign-in details and device sessions are removed from our active systems within 30 days.
  • Favourites are kept with your account and deleted with it.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 90 days for security and troubleshooting.
  • Exported spreadsheets live only on your device and wherever you shared them.

7. Security

All traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, and your session token is stored on your device and revoked when you sign out. The server checks your permissions on every request, so the App can never show more than you could see by signing in to the website. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data using appropriate technical and organisational measures.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access and update – your name and role are shown in the App. To change them, ask your business's manager or contact us.
  • Sign out – sign out from the App to end the session on that device and revoke its token on the server.
  • Delete – ask for your account and data to be deleted (see below).
  • Portability, restriction and objection – depending on where you live you may have further rights, including to receive a copy of your data or to object to certain processing. To exercise any right, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days and may ask you to verify your identity first.

9. Deleting your account

Staff accounts are created and managed by your business, so deletion is handled by request rather than from inside the App.

By request: ask your business's owner or manager to delete your staff account on yeaksa.com, or email us at myyeaksa@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will verify your identity, deactivate the account immediately and erase your personal data from our active systems within 30 days. You can also sign out of the App at any time, which ends the session on your device.

Business records your employer is legally required to retain are kept by the business for the statutory period; they are not part of your account.

10. Children

The App is intended for adult staff only. You must be at least 18 years old to hold a staff account. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located outside your country, including in countries that do not offer the same level of data protection as your own. Whenever we transfer data internationally we take steps to ensure it remains protected in line with this policy.

12. Third-party links

The App connects only to the server your business runs on (yeaksa.com or mekhea.com). Sharing an export opens the Android share sheet; the app you choose there has its own privacy policy.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. If the changes are significant we will notify you in the App before they take effect. Continued use of the App after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.
