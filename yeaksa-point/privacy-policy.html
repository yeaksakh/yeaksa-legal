Privacy Policy

Yeaksa Point (ពិន្ទុយក្សា) mobile application (Android package com.yeaksa.yeaksapoint) · Effective date: 17 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yeaksa ("Yeaksa", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use the Yeaksa Point mobile application (the "App"). Yeaksa Point is the loyalty-points app of the Yeaksa marketplace (yeaksa.com): it holds your Yeaksa points balance, lets you send points to other Yeaksa Point users and request points from them, top up your points through a Yeaksa agent or a bank transfer, redeem points through a Yeaksa agent, and pay with points by scanning a QR code. By installing or using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

In short: we collect what is needed to open and verify your points account, move points between accounts, and keep the account safe. We do not sell your personal data, and the App contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. Your contacts are read only on your device to help you pick a recipient; the list is never uploaded. You can delete your account from inside the App at any time (see Deleting your account).

1. Who we are

The App is operated by Yeaksa, an online marketplace based in Khan Russey Keo, Phnom Penh, Cambodia. Yeaksa is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. Contact details are in the Contact us section.

3. App permissions

The App may ask for the following Android permissions. Each one is only needed for the feature described and can be refused; the rest of the App keeps working:

  • Camera – to scan a QR code when paying, and to photograph your ID and take your selfie during verification. The camera plugin also declares the microphone permission; the App never records audio.
  • Contacts – to pick a recipient from your phone book. Read on the device only.
  • Notifications – to alert you to incoming points and requests.
  • Photos / storage – to choose a profile photo or an ID photo from your gallery and to save a transaction receipt.
  • Biometrics (fingerprint / face) – to unlock the App instead of typing your PIN, if you turn it on.
  • Phone state – to identify the device your account is bound to.
  • Internet – to connect to our servers.

4. How we use your data

  • To open, verify and secure your points account and sign you in.
  • To carry out the transactions you ask for and show you your balance and history.
  • To notify you about incoming points, requests and security events.
  • To detect and prevent fraud and abuse, and to enforce our terms.
  • To comply with legal obligations, including record-keeping and requests from competent authorities.
  • To improve the App using aggregated information that does not identify you.

5. Who we share data with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only as follows:

  • Other users – when you send or request points, the other party sees your name and phone number, and you see theirs.
  • Agents – when you top up or redeem points through a Yeaksa agent, the agent sees your name, phone number and the amount.
  • Payment providers – when you top up points through a payment provider, you enter your payment details on the provider's own page; they process it under their own privacy policy.
  • Service providers – companies that host our servers, send SMS codes and deliver push notifications (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging). They may only use your data to provide the service to us.
  • Legal requirements – if required by law, court order or a competent authority, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Yeaksa, our users or others.
  • Business transfers – if Yeaksa is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and verification data is kept while your account exists and for up to 30 days after you delete it, except as required below.
  • Transaction records may be kept for up to 10 years where required by tax and accounting law, even after your account is closed.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 90 days for security and troubleshooting.

7. Security

All traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. PINs are stored only as salted hashes; the PIN used for biometric unlock is kept in your device's secure storage. Your session token is stored on your device and revoked when you sign out. Access to personal data is restricted to staff who need it to run the service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data using appropriate technical and organisational measures.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access and update – view and edit your profile in the App under Profile.
  • Notifications – disable them in your device's app settings.
  • Permissions – revoke camera, contacts or biometric access at any time in your device settings; the related feature stops working and the rest of the App continues.
  • Delete – delete your account from inside the App (see below).
  • Portability, restriction and objection – depending on where you live you may have further rights, including to receive a copy of your data or to object to certain processing. To exercise any right, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days and may ask you to verify your identity first.

9. Deleting your account

In the App: open Profile → Delete Account and confirm. Your account is deactivated immediately and your personal data is erased from our active systems within 30 days. Any remaining points must be redeemed before deletion.

By request: email us at myyeaksa@gmail.com from the phone number or email on your account and ask us to delete it. We will verify your identity and do the same.

Transaction records that we are legally required to retain (see section 6) are kept for the statutory period and then destroyed. Everything else is deleted.

10. Children

You must be at least 18 years old to open a Yeaksa Point account. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located outside your country, including in countries that do not offer the same level of data protection as your own. Whenever we transfer data internationally we take steps to ensure it remains protected in line with this policy.

12. Third-party links

Topping up through a payment provider opens that provider's page inside the App. Those services have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for how they handle your data.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. If the changes are significant we will notify you in the App or by notification before they take effect. Continued use of the App after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.
