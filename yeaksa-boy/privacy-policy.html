Privacy Policy

YeaksaBoy (ដឹកជញ្ជូនយក្សា) mobile application (Android package com.yeaksa.delivery_boy) · Effective date: 16 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yeaksa ("Yeaksa", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use the YeaksaBoy mobile application (the "App"). YeaksaBoy is the companion app for delivery riders who deliver orders placed on our online store at yeaksa.com: it shows a rider the deliveries assigned to them, walks each delivery through pickup and hand-over, and records the proof of delivery. It is intended for riders who work with Yeaksa; rider accounts are created by Yeaksa, not through the App. By installing or using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

In short: we collect the information needed to sign you in as a rider, assign deliveries to you, record where and when each parcel was handed over, and pay you. We do not sell your personal data, and the App contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. Your location is used only at the moment you confirm a delivery step, never in the background. You can ask us to delete your rider account at any time (see Deleting your account).

1. Who we are

The App is operated by Yeaksa, an online marketplace based in Khan Russey Keo, Phnom Penh, Cambodia. Yeaksa is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. Contact details are in the Contact us section.

2. Data we collect

We only collect data that you give us or that is needed to run the delivery service. A rider account is required to use the App; it is created for you by Yeaksa when you start delivering for us.

3. App permissions

The App may ask for the following Android permissions. Each one is requested only when you use the related feature:

  • Location (fine/coarse, while in use) – to record your position at the moment you confirm a pickup or hand-over. If you refuse, the delivery can still be completed; no position is recorded.
  • Camera – to take the proof-of-delivery photo. The system camera is used, so we receive only the photo you take. The App does not read your gallery.
  • Notifications – to alert you when a new delivery is assigned while you are online. You can turn these off in your device settings at any time; you will then only see new deliveries when you open the App.
  • Internet – to connect to our servers.

Tapping a customer's phone number or address hands off to your phone's dialler or maps app; the App itself does not make calls or access your call history.

4. How we use your data

  • To sign you in and keep your session on your device.
  • To assign deliveries to you and show you the pickup, drop-off and customer details needed to complete them.
  • To record the progress of each delivery, the proof-of-delivery photo and the hand-over location, so the customer can track their parcel and the shop can resolve any dispute.
  • To alert you to new deliveries while you are online.
  • To calculate and show your earnings, delivery rate and rating, and to pay you.
  • To keep the App secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our terms.
  • To comply with legal obligations such as tax, accounting and labour law.
  • To improve the App based on how its features are used, using aggregated information that does not identify you.

5. Who we share data with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only as follows:

  • Customers – the customer whose order you are delivering may see your name, your photo if we have one, and the status of their delivery, so they know who is bringing their parcel.
  • Sellers on the marketplace – the seller whose parcel you collect can see the delivery status, the hand-over location and the proof-of-delivery photo for their own orders.
  • Service providers – companies that host our servers. They may only use your data to provide the service to us.
  • Legal requirements – if required by law, court order or a competent authority, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Yeaksa, our users or others.
  • Business transfers – if Yeaksa is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

In turn, the App shows you customers' names, phone numbers and addresses so that you can deliver to them. You may use that information only to complete the delivery, and you must not copy, keep or share it for any other purpose.

6. How long we keep data

  • Rider account data is kept while you deliver for Yeaksa. When your account is closed, your profile, sign-in details and device session are removed from our active systems within 30 days.
  • Delivery records – the status history, hand-over location, proof-of-delivery photo and the earnings for each order – are part of the order and may be kept for up to 10 years where required by tax, accounting and consumer-protection law, even after your account is closed, in a form that is no longer linked to a usable profile.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 90 days for security and troubleshooting.

7. Security

All traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Sign-in PINs are stored only as salted hashes, and your session token is stored on your device and revoked when you sign out. Access to personal data is restricted to staff who need it to run the service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data using appropriate technical and organisational measures.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access and update – you can view your name, phone number, vehicle and rating in the App under Profile. To change them, contact Yeaksa using the details below.
  • Notifications – disable them in your device's app settings, or switch yourself offline in the App so that no new deliveries are sent to you.
  • Permissions – revoke location or camera access at any time in your device settings; the related feature will stop working but the rest of the App will continue to function.
  • Delete – ask us to close your rider account and delete your data (see below).
  • Portability, restriction and objection – depending on where you live you may have further rights, including to receive a copy of your data or to object to certain processing. To exercise any right, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days and may ask you to verify your identity first.

9. Deleting your account

Rider accounts are created and managed by Yeaksa, so deletion is handled by request rather than from inside the App.

By request: email us at myyeaksa@gmail.com, or contact us from the phone number registered to your rider account, and ask us to delete your account. We will verify your identity, deactivate the account immediately and erase your personal data from our active systems within 30 days. You can also sign out of the App at any time under Profile → Sign out, which ends the session on your device and removes the locally stored data.

Delivery records that we are legally required to retain (see section 6) are kept for the statutory period and then destroyed. Everything else is deleted.

10. Children

The App is intended for adult delivery riders only. You must be at least 18 years old to hold a rider account. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located outside your country, including in countries that do not offer the same level of data protection as your own. Whenever we transfer data internationally we take steps to ensure it remains protected in line with this policy.

12. Third-party links

The App may open other apps on your phone – your dialler to call a customer, or a maps app to navigate to an address. Those services have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for how they handle your data.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. If the changes are significant we will notify you in the App or by notification before they take effect. Continued use of the App after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.
