Privacy Policy

Yeaksacturing (ផលិតកម្មយក្សា) mobile application (Android package com.yeaksa.manufacturing) · Effective date: 17 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yeaksa ("Yeaksa", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use the Yeaksacturing mobile application (the "App"). Yeaksacturing is the factory-floor app for businesses that run their operations on the Yeaksa platform (yeaksa.com): staff record production runs and damages, count stock by scanning, and use the HR features their business has enabled – clocking in and out, leave, holidays and payslips. It is intended for the staff of those businesses, not for shoppers. By installing or using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

In short: you sign in with the staff account your business created on yeaksa.com. The App shows and records your business's own production, stock and HR data on the Yeaksa servers. We do not sell personal data, the App contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and you can have your account and data deleted at any time (see Deleting your account).

1. Who we are

The App is operated by Yeaksa, an online marketplace and business platform based in Khan Russey Keo, Phnom Penh, Cambodia. Yeaksa is the data controller for the account and technical data described in this policy. The business that employs you is the controller of the production, stock and HR records it keeps about you; Yeaksa processes those records on its behalf. Contact details are in the Contact us section.

2. Data we collect

You cannot use the App without an account: a business owner or manager creates staff accounts on yeaksa.com and gives you a username and password. The App then only shows and records data belonging to that business.

3. App permissions

The App may ask for the following Android permissions. Each one is only needed for the feature described and can be refused; the rest of the App keeps working:

  • Camera – to scan product barcodes when counting stock, and to take the optional attendance selfie. The App does not read your photo gallery.
  • Location (approximate, while in use) – to record where you clocked in or out. Refusing it never blocks a punch.
  • Internet – to connect to your business's server.

4. How we use your data

  • To sign you in and keep your session on your device.
  • To record production runs, damages and stock counts for your business, and to show you recipes, products and stock figures.
  • To record your attendance, handle leave requests and approvals, show holidays, and show your payslips.
  • To keep the App secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our terms.
  • To comply with legal obligations such as tax, accounting and labour law.
  • To improve the App based on how its features are used, using aggregated information that does not identify you.

5. Who we share data with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only as follows:

  • Your business – the owners and managers of the business you work for can see the records you create and your attendance, leave and payroll data, on yeaksa.com and in the Yeaksa apps. That is the purpose of the service.
  • Service providers – companies that host our servers. They may only use your data to provide the service to us.
  • Legal requirements – if required by law, court order or a competent authority, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Yeaksa, our users or others.
  • Business transfers – if Yeaksa is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data is kept while your staff account exists. When it is deleted, your profile, sign-in details and device sessions are removed from our active systems within 30 days.
  • Production, stock and attendance records belong to your business and are kept as long as the business keeps them, and for up to 10 years where required by tax, accounting and labour law, in a form that no longer links to a usable profile once your account is gone.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 90 days for security and troubleshooting.

7. Security

All traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, and your session token is stored on your device and revoked when you sign out. Access to personal data is restricted to staff who need it to run the service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data using appropriate technical and organisational measures.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access and update – your name, role and branch are shown in the App under Profile. To change them, ask your business's manager or contact us.
  • Permissions – revoke camera or location access at any time in your device settings; the related feature stops working and the rest of the App continues.
  • Sign out – Profile → Sign out ends the session on that device and revokes its token on the server.
  • Delete – ask for your account and data to be deleted (see below).
  • Portability, restriction and objection – depending on where you live you may have further rights, including to receive a copy of your data or to object to certain processing. To exercise any right, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days and may ask you to verify your identity first.

9. Deleting your account

Staff accounts are created and managed by your business, so deletion is handled by request rather than from inside the App.

By request: ask your business's owner or manager to delete your staff account on yeaksa.com, or email us at myyeaksa@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will verify your identity, deactivate the account immediately and erase your personal data from our active systems within 30 days. You can also sign out at any time under Profile → Sign out, which ends the session on your device.

Records that your business is legally required to retain (see section 6) are kept for the statutory period and then destroyed. Everything else is deleted.

10. Children

The App is intended for adult staff only. You must be at least 18 years old to hold a staff account. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located outside your country, including in countries that do not offer the same level of data protection as your own. Whenever we transfer data internationally we take steps to ensure it remains protected in line with this policy.

12. Third-party links

The App connects only to the server your business runs on (yeaksa.com or mekhea.com) and contains no links to third-party websites or services.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. If the changes are significant we will notify you in the App before they take effect. Continued use of the App after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.
