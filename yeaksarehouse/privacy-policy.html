Privacy Policy

Yeaksarehouse (ឃ្លាំងយក្សា) mobile application (Android package com.yeaksa.warehouse) · Effective date: 17 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yeaksa ("Yeaksa", "we", "us", "our") handles personal data in the Yeaksarehouse mobile application (the "App"). Yeaksarehouse is a warehouse-floor app for staff of shops that sell on yeaksa.com: it moves customer orders through the preparing and checking stages, lets staff pick orders by scanning items, and keeps stock levels and stock counts. It is intended for warehouse staff, not for shoppers. By installing or using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

In short: the current version of the App works entirely on your device. It does not connect to the internet, has no user accounts on our servers, asks for no device permissions, and contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. The only data it keeps is what you enter on the handset (your staff code, the orders and stock you work on, and a count in progress), and it is stored on that device alone. You can erase it at any time by signing out or clearing the App's storage (see Deleting your data).

1. Who we are

The App is published by Yeaksa, an online marketplace based in Khan Russey Keo, Phnom Penh, Cambodia. Where personal data is processed, Yeaksa is the data controller. Contact details are in the Contact us section.

2. Data the App handles

The App does not send any data to Yeaksa or to anyone else. Everything below is stored only on the device the App is installed on, using the device's private app storage, so that a part-picked order or a half-walked stock count survives closing the App or restarting the handset.

CategoryWhat is stored on the deviceWhy
Sign-inThe staff code you type in, your name and role (packer, checker or supervisor) and the warehouse you work at. Your 4-digit PIN is used only to sign you in and is not kept after that.To show who is signed in on a shared handset and which actions (preparing, checking, sending back, adjusting stock, submitting a count) that role may perform.
OrdersThe orders in the warehouse queues: order number, the customer's name, the items and quantities ordered, how many have been picked, the stage each order is in with the time it changed, and any short-pick reason or note a staff member writes.To move orders through the preparing and checking stages and record why an order was short.
StockProduct names, SKUs, barcodes, bin locations, quantities, stock adjustments with their reason, and any stock count in progress.To search stock, adjust it and complete a count.
PreferencesWhether you are signed in, and the app follows your device's light/dark setting.To keep you signed in between launches.

In this version the orders and stock are sample data built into the App. The App does not collect your contacts, location, photos, files, call logs, messages, advertising identifiers, biometric data or any device identifier, and it makes no network requests at all.

If a future version connects to a Yeaksa business server so that real orders and stock are synchronised, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the App will only ever send the categories listed above.

3. App permissions

The App requests no Android permissions. It does not use the camera, location, storage, contacts, microphone, Bluetooth or the internet. Barcodes are scanned with a keyboard-wedge scanner (a scanner that types the code into the App like a keyboard) or typed by hand.

4. How the data is used

  • To sign staff in and out on a shared handset and apply the right role rules.
  • To show the Prepare, Check and Driver queues and move orders through them.
  • To record what was picked on each order and why a pick was short.
  • To search, adjust and count stock.
  • To restore a part-picked order, a count in progress and the signed-in session after a restart.

Because nothing leaves the device, Yeaksa itself cannot see this data and does not use it for any other purpose.

5. Who we share data with

We do not share, sell or transfer any data from the App. The App has no server connection and no third-party SDKs, so no data reaches Yeaksa, advertisers, analytics providers or anyone else. The only way data leaves the handset is if the shop's own staff read it from the screen.

The App shows staff the customer's name on each order so the parcel can be packed for the right person. Staff may use that information only to fulfil the order.

6. How long data is kept

  • The signed-in session is kept on the device until you sign out.
  • Orders, stock, adjustments and a count in progress stay on the device until they are completed, replaced, or the App's storage is cleared or the App is uninstalled.
  • Yeaksa holds no copy of any of it.

7. Security

Data is stored in the App's private storage on the device, which other apps cannot read. Handsets are usually shared, so sign out when you hand a device over, and protect the device with a screen lock. Because the data never travels over a network, there is nothing to intercept in transit.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access – everything the App stores about you is visible in the App under Profile, Orders, Stock and Count.
  • Erase – sign out (Profile → Sign out) or clear the App's storage in your device settings (see below).
  • Questions – if you believe Yeaksa holds personal data about you in connection with the App, or you want to exercise any right you have under the law where you live, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Deleting your data

The App has no account on Yeaksa's servers, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To remove all of the App's data from a handset:

  • In the App: open Profile → Sign out. This ends the session on that device.
  • On the device: open Android Settings → Apps → Yeaksarehouse → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the App. This erases the session, orders, stock and any count in progress immediately and permanently.

Staff codes and roles are issued by the shop that employs you. To have a staff code withdrawn, ask your supervisor, or email us at myyeaksa@gmail.com and we will pass the request on within 30 days.

10. Children

The App is intended for adult warehouse staff only. You must be at least 18 years old to be issued a staff code. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used the App and you need help, please contact us.

11. International transfers

The App does not transfer data anywhere, so no international transfer takes place. If a future version synchronises with Yeaksa servers, those servers and our service providers may be located outside your country, and we will take steps to ensure your data remains protected in line with this policy.

12. Third-party links

The App contains no links to third-party websites or services and opens no other apps.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, in particular when the App gains a server connection. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. If the changes are significant we will tell you in the App before they take effect. Continued use of the App after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.
